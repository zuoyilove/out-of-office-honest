A tiny deterministic program that writes one painfully honest out-of-office auto-reply to `entry.txt`: away until Monday, with your email joining 312 unread messages avoided since Tuesday, and a promise to reply once back, rested, and brave enough to open the inbox.

## The reply

> I'm away until Monday, and honestly your email will join the 312 unread messages I've been avoiding since Tuesday — I'll reply once I'm back, rested, and brave enough to open my inbox.

One sentence, 184 characters. The program writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (`./output/entry.txt` by default), with no trailing newline.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
